<!-- ABOUT THE PROJECT -->
<h2 id="about-the-project" style="border-bottom: 3px solid #667eea; padding-bottom: 10px; display: inline-block;">
  About The Project
</h2>

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #1e293b 0%, #0f172a 100%); padding: 25px; border-radius: 16px; border: 1px solid #334155; margin: 20px 0;">
  <p style="color: #cbd5e1; line-height: 1.8;">
<strong>"Islamic History Digital Platform"</strong> is a comprehensive web application of the 
<strong style="color: #667eea;">Department of Islamic History</strong> at Ananda Mohan University College. 
It functions simultaneously as the department’s notice board, social platform, and admin dashboard.
  </p>
  
  <br />

  <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 20px;">
    <div style="background: rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.1); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; border: 1px solid rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.2);">
      <h3 style="color: #667eea; margin-top: 0;">Mission</h3>
      <p style="color: #94a3b8; font-size: 0.95rem;">To facilitate easier information exchange between students and teachers of the department through digital means and to build an interactive community.
</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background: rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; border: 1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2);">
      <h3 style="color: #8b5cf6; margin-top: 0;">Vision</h3>
      <p style="color: #94a3b8; font-size: 0.95rem;">To play a leading role in the digital transformation of educational institutions and make the education system more dynamic through modern technology.
</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
